# Owner inputs

The storefront is an enquiry-only catalogue. No stock, prices or shipping dates have been invented.

Supply the seller name, business address, monitored customer-support contact and the privacy/retention decisions appropriate to the actual business. Confirm applicable legal requirements before publishing policies; this project is not a legal compliance certification.

For each physical diary, confirm trim size, binding margin, pages/leaves, paper GSM and ruling, cover material/finish, price, taxes where applicable, and sample approval. Confirm courier serviceability, costs, lead times and return/cancellation terms before accepting orders. There is no approved 48-hour replacement rule.

Optional integrations require your own Supabase project, Google OAuth provider, GA4 measurement ID and Cloudflare analytics token. Never place service-role keys or OAuth secrets in PUBLIC environment variables. See OAUTH_SETUP_GUIDE.md and LAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md.
