# Free-tier planning

Checked 17 October 2026. Service policies can change; review the linked provider pages before launch.

- Cloudflare Pages Free: 500 builds per month; static hosting limits and file-size restrictions apply. Source: https://developers.cloudflare.com/pages/platform/limits/
- Supabase Free: 50,000 monthly active users, 500 MB database, 1 GB file storage, 5 GB egress plus 5 GB cached egress. Two active free projects; projects can pause after a week of inactivity. No automatic backups/PITR on Free. API requests are listed as unlimited, subject to resource and rate limits. Source: https://supabase.com/pricing
- GA4 standard has collection/configuration limits; the old Universal Analytics 10-million-hits figure is not a GA4 monthly event allowance. Source: https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/9267744

This static catalogue has no paid checkout, transactional email or server-function requirement. Domain renewal remains a separate Namecheap cost. A free-tier architecture is not a guarantee that every future usage pattern or add-on will be free. Review usage dashboards and do not enable paid upgrades without a deliberate decision.
